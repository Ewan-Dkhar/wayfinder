# Lady Butterfly Combat Guide

## Phase 1 Tactics
Lady Butterfly uses acrobatic wire leaps and shuriken barrages.
Whenever she leaps into the air onto a high wire, throw a standard Shuriken immediately.
Hitting her mid-air will knock her off the wire directly to the ground for heavy posture buildup and free follow-up vitality strikes.

## Phase 2 Illusions and Phantom Butterflies
In Phase 2, she summons phantom soldiers around the temple.
Sprint in wide circles around the outer pillars until the phantoms turn into glowing homing butterflies.
Hide behind a solid wooden pillar to absorb all butterfly damage.
Once clear, rush her aggressively and use Nightjar Slash to prevent her from summoning more illusions.
